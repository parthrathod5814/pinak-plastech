About PINAK PLASTECH

PINAK PLASTECH is a trusted manufacturer of PP Hollow Sheets and customised industrial plastic packaging solutions, serving customers across 10+ countries worldwide. With a strong focus on manufacturing quality, product customisation and reliable supply, we deliver solutions for construction, ceiling, packaging, beverage, tile protection, cable and industrial applications.

Our in-house manufacturing capabilities enable us to meet diverse customer requirements with flexible specifications, consistent production standards and application-focused solutions. From domestic industries to international markets, we are committed to building long-term partnerships through dependable products and customer-focused service.
